# Content quality audit — all vocabulary data files

Generated by `node scripts/_diag_wordquality.js` (add `--samples`, `--rule CODE`,
`--lang xx`, `--json out.json`). Re-run it after any bulk data edit.

**Scope:** 43 vocabulary files, 115,859 entries, 6 languages, levels A1–C2 plus
the TOEFL / phrasal-verb / separable-verb / synonym-antonym sets.
The 4 `readingcomp*.js` files are not vocabulary lists and are excluded.

**Headline:** 35,945 of 115,859 entries (**31.0%**) carry at least one content
defect.

| language | entries | affected | rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| de | 36,746 | 18,926 | **52%** |
| en | 56,487 | 14,062 | 25% |
| fr | 21,504 | 2,899 | 13% |
| it | 360 | 30 | 8% |
| es | 361 | 19 | 5% |
| pt | 401 | 9 | 2% |

Structure is sound — zero missing separators, zero empty translation halves,
zero exact duplicate headwords, zero broken markup of consequence. Every finding
below is about **content**, not format.

## 1. Missing example sentences — 20,240 entries (17.5%)

`E_FALLBACK`. The example field is the literal placeholder
`"No example sentence available"` / `"Beispielsatz nicht verfügbar"` etc.
Affects every game that quizzes on a sentence (Cloze, Dictation, Sentence

## 2. Placeholder definitions masquerading as real ones — 4,327 entries

`D_SHARED`. Two strings alone account for 4,327 entries:

- **1,665 of 2,748** entries in `partikelverbde.js` (61%) have the definition
  `"German separable verb from an open-source word list."` — that is a data
  provenance note, not a meaning. 61% of the German separable-verb deck teaches
  nothing.
- **2,662** entries across all six `words*gode.js` files have the definition
  `"weak/mixed all-case plural"` — a grammar label, not a meaning:
  wordsc2gode 1,136 · wordsc1gode 773 · wordsb2gode 497 · wordsb1gode 197 ·
  wordsa2gode 50 · wordsa1gode 6.

The second one is worse than it looks, because those headwords are **not lemmas**:

```js
word: "mühsamen",                 // declined form of "mühsam"
pos: "adjective", level: "C2",
definition: "weak/mixed all-case plural - zayıf/karışık her durumda çoğul",
example: "Die betagte Dame schleppte sich mühsam die Treppe hinauf. - …",
```

Same defect class as the Wiktionary form-of problem already fixed in
`synantde.js` / `toefl.js` / `partikelverbde.js`, but 2,662 entries wide and
still live. The example sentence also shows the *base* form, so the learner is
drilled on a word that never appears.

The remaining ~140 `D_SHARED` hits are benign — genuine spelling-variant
clusters in English C1/C2 (`borscht/borsch/borsh/borsht/borshch/bortsch`,
`acclimatise/acclimatize/…`). Still corpus bloat, but not wrong.

## 3. Same word taught at multiple CEFR levels — 5,119 entries (4.4%)

`W_CROSSLEVEL`. Almost entirely German:

| file | affected | rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| wordsa2gode.js (de A2) | 985 | **51%** |
| wordsb1gode.js (de B1) | 1,048 | 36% |
| wordsc2gode.js (de C2) | 1,731 | 23% |
| wordsb2gode.js (de B2) | 339 | 7% |
| wordsa1.js (en A1) | 269 | 18% |
| wordsa2.js (en A2) | 279 | 13% |

Examples: `ich` taught at A1 **and C2**; `Bitte` at A1 + B2 + C2; `alt`,
`teuer`, `glücklich`, `müde`, `der Regen`, `die Wohnung` all at A1 + A2.
Half the German A2 deck is a repeat of another level, so level selection
does not mean what the user thinks it means.

## 4. Definitions that are not definitions — 6,199 entries

**`D_SYNLIST` — 2,769 entries, all in `synantde.js` (29% of that file).**
The definition is a synonym list:

```
ausbetten → "Ähnlich wie: exhumieren, umbetten.;Şuna benzer: mezardan çıkarma, yeniden gömme."
gabelig   → "Ähnlich wie: gespalten.;Şuna benzer: bölünmüş."
```

**`D_CIRCULAR` — 3,430 entries.** A short definition that just restates the
headword. Two distinct sub-problems:

- `synantde.js` 2,327 · `synanten.js` 108 — overlaps the synonym-list issue.
- **French: the definition is often just the English cognate**, which teaches
  nothing to a Turkish learner:
  `possible → "possible."`, `long → "long."`, `important → "important."`,
  `secret → "secret."`, `impossible → "impossible."`
  (fr total 431: B1 106 · C2 95 · B2 85 · C1 66 · A2 59 · A1 20).
- English C1/C2/TOEFL: Wiktionary noun-sense dumps under the wrong part of
  speech — `abroad → "Countries or lands abroad."`,
  `accipiter → "Any hawk of the genus Accipiter."`,
  `accusative → "(grammar) The accusative case."`,
  `running → "The activity or sport of running."`

## 5. Example sentence does not contain the word — 2,423 entries (2.1%)

`E_NOWORD`, after allowing for regular inflection, irregular forms, and the
definite article stored inside `word`. Worst: partikelverbde 483 (17%),
fr B1 580, fr B2 365, fr C2 327, fr C1 282, fr A2 273, de C2 354, synantde 329.

Genuinely wrong pairings, e.g. `l'été` ("summer") illustrated with
*"C'est toujours tel que ça a été."* — where `été` is the past participle of
*être*, a different word entirely.

## 6. One example sentence shared by many words — 404 entries

`E_SHARED`, English only: wordsc2 155 · toefl 118 · wordsc1 114 · wordsb2 17.
One sentence is reused verbatim for up to 10 unrelated headwords
(*"Fats are broken down into fatty acids and glycerol."* × 10 in TOEFL;
*"Do you know what benzyl acetate smells like?"* for
`acetal, acetals, acetate, acetates, benzylic, benzyl`).

## Suggested order of work

1. **`partikelverbde.js` provenance-note definitions** (1,665) — one file, one
   string, highest damage-per-fix ratio.
2. **German declined-form headwords** (2,662) — same pipeline as
   `scripts/fix_synant_formof.py`: fold each inflected form into its lemma or
   drop it. Also fixes a large share of the `E_NOWORD` and `D_SHARED` counts.
3. **German cross-level duplication** (5,119) — keep the lowest level, drop the
   rest; makes level selection meaningful again.
4. **`synantde.js` synonym-list definitions** (2,769) — real glosses instead of
   `Ähnlich wie: …`.
5. **French cognate definitions** (431) — cheap, high visibility, A1/A2 first.
6. **Missing examples** (20,240) — biggest number but most expensive; the
   Tatoeba bulk-export + local-match approach used previously applies.
7. **Long tail** — `phrasalverbsfr.js` categories, `W_JUNK` slash-forms,
   `E_MULTISEP`, mojibake: a few hundred entries total, mostly mechanical.
