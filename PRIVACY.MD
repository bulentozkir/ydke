# YDKE Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 14 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the native Windows application **YDKE - Yabanci Dil Kelime Ezberleme** ("YDKE", "the app", "we", or "us"). It applies to the current native YDKE application, not the retired web app or former website.

## Summary

- YDKE is a local-only app.
- No account, sign-in, browser handoff, or cloud sync is required or available.
- YDKE can be used offline after installation.
- Study data is stored on the Windows device.
- YDKE has no advertising, ad tracking, analytics, or profiling code.
- The app does not need a child name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or account to provide Cards, Quiz, Words, or Games.

## Information Stored Locally

YDKE stores app data in local Windows app storage. This can include:

- interface language, study language, level, text-size, and appearance preferences;
- daily practice goal, reduced-motion preference, and untimed-practice preference;
- favorite and marked-known vocabulary items;
- card review history, quiz/game results, streaks, daily activity, and resumable sessions; and
- recovery information used to perform safe restore operations.

The vocabulary datasets are bundled with the app package and loaded from local files.

If the application encounters an unhandled error, it may write a local `crash.log` file containing the date/time and technical exception details. YDKE does not upload this file.

## Local Backups

In **Settings > For grown-ups**:

- **Make a copy** exports a local backup file selected by the user.
- **Open a copy...** restores a local backup file chosen by the user.

Before restore, YDKE validates the backup, asks for confirmation, and creates a safety copy. Backup files are under user control at the selected location.

## Network and Third Parties

Core learning features are local and do not send ordinary study activity to YDKE servers.

The current native app does not provide Google sign-in, Firebase sync, or cloud backup features.

Windows and Microsoft Store services may process technical data under Microsoft terms when the app is installed, updated, or used. YDKE does not include a separate advertising or analytics SDK.

For speech playback, YDKE uses installed Windows speech voices on the device. YDKE does not send spoken text to a YDKE server.

## Children and Family Use

YDKE is designed for children ages 6-12 and does not require personal information for core learning features.

YDKE does not intentionally request a child name, phone number, home address, date of birth, or school information.

Parents or guardians should supervise backup export/import and reset actions on shared family devices.

This section describes product behavior. The publisher should obtain legal review for jurisdictions where YDKE is distributed, including child-directed requirements.

## What YDKE Does Not Do

The native YDKE app does not:

- require an account to study;
- sell personal data or learning data;
- show advertisements;
- load ad, analytics, or behavioral-tracking scripts;
- auto-upload local learning progress; or
- upload local crash logs through YDKE services.

## Retention and Deletion

Local settings, progress, backups, and crash logs remain on the device until the user removes them, uninstalls/resets the app, or replaces them through restore actions.

Local backup files saved outside app storage must be deleted by the user from the selected save location.

Because the current native app has no cloud account/sync service, there is no YDKE cloud profile to delete.

## Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the publisher support contact listed in the official YDKE Microsoft Store listing or distribution package. Before public release, the publisher should ensure that contact is monitored for privacy inquiries.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when data practices or legal requirements change. Material updates will revise the effective date at the top.
